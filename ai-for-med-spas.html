These are potential applications, not a prebuilt product catalogue. Feasibility and responsibilities are checked for each workflow.

01 / First response

Inquiry assistance

Draft routine answers from approved clinic information and route unclear requests to the right person.

Business measureActual response time and unresolved inquiries.

02 / Booking

Booking coordination

Support the next booking step and make incomplete requests visible to reception. Confirm appointments only through supported system actions.

Business measureBooking completion and attended consultations.

03 / Follow-through

Follow-up support

Prepare appropriate administrative follow-up and flag cases awaiting staff action. Staff approve the communication rules.

Business measureOpen follow-up tasks and staff effort.

04 / Information

Staff knowledge support

Help staff find approved operational guidance, with an escalation path when information is missing or uncertain.

Business measureTime spent finding information and required corrections.

05 / Administration

Workflow assistance

Classify routine requests, prepare summaries and move approved tasks through supported software connections.

Business measureTime per task, rework and exceptions.

06 / Visibility

Reporting support

Organize authorized records and flag discrepancies for a named reviewer before leadership uses the figures.

Business measureReconciliation time and unresolved differences.

A little more clarity

Before approving
an AI workflow.

Can this work with our current CRM or booking software?

Compatibility depends on supported integrations, vendor terms and permitted data access. The scope specifies what a connection can read or change before implementation.

Is this an AI receptionist product?

Mirèl provides business diagnosis, workflow design and scoped implementation. Any reception workflow is designed around the practice’s needs, supported tools and team responsibilities.

Can AI make treatment recommendations?

Clinical assessment and treatment suitability remain with qualified professionals. The workflows described here focus on defined nonclinical tasks and staff support.

How do we judge whether AI is helping?

Measure the task and the wider outcome: actual responses, attended consultations, staff time, exceptions and total operating cost. A fixed financial return is not guaranteed.
