What the example suggests

Coverage improved.

More eligible inquiries received a reply and more consultations were attended. The team also spent more time on review and follow-up.

What remains unresolved

Net value needs checking.

The report still needs delivery costs, operating costs and an assessment of other influences. The figures alone do not establish that AI caused the change.
