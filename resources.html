From Inquiry to Consultation

Use this guide to review who owns the first response, where booking gets difficult and whether each inquiry has a clear next step.

Start with the inquiries you already receive. Follow a few through the current workflow and note where the response, booking or follow-up stalls.

  1. 01 / Map
    Identify who owns the response.

    Record where inquiries arrive, who responds and who takes over when the primary owner is unavailable.

  2. 02 / Inspect
    Locate the booking friction.

    Review response delays, unanswered questions and any steps that require patients or staff to repeat work.

  3. 03 / Prioritize
    Clarify the next step.

    Choose one handoff to examine and what to track before changing it, such as response coverage or completed bookings.
