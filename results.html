Our Commitment

We show the work we can demonstrate—not results we have not earned.

These are concept studies, visual explorations and strategic directions. They show how we think and what we would build; they are not represented as paying-client case studies, campaign results or verified increases in bookings.

Market context is different from agency performance. For example, the British Association of Aesthetic Plastic Surgeons' 2025 UK audit reported 26,840 cosmetic surgery procedures . This is a surgical audit—not a measure of med spa bookings or Mirel client outcomes.
Why This Matters

Trust begins before the first consultation. The proof we present should meet the same standard we recommend to your clinic.

The Mirel Standard

Your clinic should never feel interchangeable online.

A premium standard of care deserves a clear, distinctive presentation—not generic ads or a website that leaves patients guessing.

We combine strategic positioning, patient psychology, conversion design and AI-assisted systems where appropriate. Human judgment and the patient's trust remain central.

A clinic's digital experience should reflect the quality of care patients receive in person.
