What We Measure

A higher standard of accountability.

An established med spa should be able to distinguish marketing activity from commercial improvement. These are the indicators we would establish and review with the clinic, subject to accessible, reliable data.

01

Inquiry quality

Which channels generate relevant inquiries? How quickly does the team respond, and how many become meaningful conversations?

02

Booking conversion

How many qualified inquiries become scheduled and attended consultations? Where is momentum lost?

03

Patient value

How often do appropriate consultations become paid treatments? What revenue is actually collected?

04

Retention & contribution

What brings patients back, and how much incremental contribution remains after the costs of growth?

Measurement framework, not a claim of historical Mirèl client performance. Data availability and attribution methods vary by clinic.

Our Evidence Standard

Before calling it a result, we want to see the evidence.

A credible performance story must show the starting position, the intervention and the commercial outcome — including uncertainty and the costs involved.

01

Establish the baseline

Define the date range, data sources, current conversion rates, service mix, patient capacity and pre-existing trends.

02

Document what changed

Record the campaigns, booking workflows, CRM processes and operational adjustments actually implemented.

03

Validate the outcome

Compare like-for-like periods, review collected revenue and costs, acknowledge other influences and seek approval before publishing any identifiable case study.
