Before we begin.

Do we need to replace our current software?

We start with the systems you already use. Supported integrations, permitted data and vendor terms determine what is feasible. A new tool is considered when its expected value justifies the cost and disruption.

Does every workflow need AI?

No. Clearer ownership or rules-based automation may solve the problem. We consider AI where interpreting or drafting information adds enough value to justify its testing, operating cost and oversight.

What stays with our team?

Your team retains the agreed approval and exception responsibilities. Clinical assessments, treatment suitability and medical recommendations remain with qualified professionals. Access, escalation and manual fallback are defined before implementation.

What happens in a Strategy Review?

A 30-minute, founder-led conversation about one operating problem. We discuss what you are seeing, which information would clarify it and whether a focused diagnostic makes sense. A diagnostic is a separate, scoped next step, subject to mutual fit.
