Do we need to replace our current software?

We start with your existing systems. Supported integrations, permitted data and vendor terms determine what is feasible. A new tool is considered when its expected value justifies the cost and disruption.

Does every workflow need AI?

No. Clearer ownership or rules-based automation may solve the problem. We consider AI where interpreting or drafting information adds enough value to justify testing, operating costs and oversight.

What stays with our team?

Your team retains agreed approval and exception responsibilities. Clinical assessments, treatment suitability and medical recommendations remain with qualified professionals. Access, escalation and manual fallback are defined before implementation.

What happens in a Strategy Review?

A 30-minute, founder-led conversation about one operating problem. We discuss what you are seeing, which information would clarify it and whether a focused diagnostic makes sense. A diagnostic is a separately scoped next step, subject to mutual fit.

Can you guarantee a financial return?

No fixed return is guaranteed. We agree a baseline, relevant measures and a review period, then assess observed results alongside costs and other factors that may have influenced performance.
