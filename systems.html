The Patient Journey

From first impression to booked consultation.

Patients rarely make a considered treatment decision from a single ad. They weigh the clinic's credibility, the clarity of its advice, practical details and whether the next step feels right.

Mirel helps those touchpoints work together—from first discovery to the consultation and, where appropriate, the follow-up that comes after.

Local trust. Local standards.

Follow-up should respect the market and the patient. The UK's ICO explains marketing email and text rules; Australia's ACMA sets out consent, sender-identification and unsubscribe requirements. Every clinic's actual process needs its own compliance review.
