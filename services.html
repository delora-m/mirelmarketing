Where should we start if we are unsure what is wrong?

Bring one recurring issue to the Strategy Review. We discuss what you are seeing and whether a separately scoped diagnostic would help.

Will we need to replace our booking software or CRM?

We start with your existing systems and check supported integrations and permitted access. A tool change needs to justify its cost and disruption.

Does every engagement include AI?

No. Clearer ownership, a process change or rules-based automation may be enough. AI is considered where it adds value to the task.

Can we start with a diagnostic without committing to a build?

Yes. The diagnostic has its own scope and outputs. You decide whether to proceed to design or implementation.

How are fees, timing and responsibilities set?

The proposal sets deliverables, responsibilities, milestones and fees, including identified third-party costs and any ongoing support.

How will we judge whether the work is paying off?

We review agreed measures against the baseline, including delivery and operating costs and other influences on performance. A fixed financial return is not guaranteed.
