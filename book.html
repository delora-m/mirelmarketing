Before We Speak

A few useful clarifications.

Is this a sales presentation?

It is an introductory strategy conversation. We want to understand the clinic's numbers, operating context and growth constraints first. If a deeper audit or paid partnership is appropriate, we will explain that separately.

Will I receive a complete audit during the call?

No. A useful audit requires supporting information and time to review the actual process. A complimentary three-day audit may be offered after the introductory call if there is a strong mutual fit and an agreed scope.

Do I need to provide CRM access or patient records before booking?

No. Initial discussion can use approximate aggregate business metrics. Please do not submit protected or identifiable patient information through the booking form. Any later system access is handled separately.

What types of med spas are the best fit?

Established clinics and growing medical aesthetics groups with meaningful patient demand, an appetite for operational improvements and a decision-maker involved in the conversation. Fit depends on economics and readiness, not only size.

Does Mirèl guarantee a specific revenue increase?

No. Responsible growth decisions begin with a baseline, realistic assumptions and a testable plan. Outcomes depend on the clinic, implementation, market conditions and factors outside any partner's control.
